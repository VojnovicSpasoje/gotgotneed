GotGotNeed

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

In short: GotGotNeed does not require an account and does not collect your collection data. Everything you enter stays on your own device. If you choose to send us feedback, we receive only what you write in that form.

Who we are

GotGotNeed is a collection tracker for albums, stickers, coins, cards and other collectibles. It is developed by Spasoje Vojnović ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the GotGotNeed website, the installable web app, and the GotGotNeed app on Google Play.

Information you enter in the app

Your collections, editions, variants, items, counts, duplicates and settings (such as your chosen theme and sort order) are stored only in your device's browser storage. They are not sent to us or to any server we control, and we cannot see them.

  • Backup and export: when you use Export, a file is created on your device. Where you keep or share that file is up to you.
  • Deleting your data: you can delete a collection in the app at any time. Uninstalling the app or clearing your browser's site data for GotGotNeed removes all of your data from that device. Because we do not hold a copy, it cannot be recovered afterwards unless you made a backup.

Feedback

If you send feedback from the app, the message type, the text you write and, only if you choose to provide it, your email address are sent to a Google Form owned by us and stored in our Google account (Google Forms and Google Sheets). We use this only to improve the app and, if you gave an email address, to reply to you. You can ask us to delete your feedback at any time.

Services that load the app

To deliver the app, your device connects to these services, which may process technical data such as your IP address and browser type under their own privacy policies:

  • GitHub Pages hosts the app files (GitHub privacy statement).
  • Google Fonts provides the typefaces used by the themes (Google privacy policy).

We do not use analytics, advertising, tracking cookies or third-party trackers, and we do not sell or share personal data.

Children

GotGotNeed is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them. Because collection data stays on the device, the app can be used without giving us any personal information.

Future accounts

We may later add optional sign-in so you can back up and sync your collections across devices. If we do, this policy will be updated before that feature is released, to explain what is stored, where, and how you can delete your account and all associated data.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. Significant changes will also be noted in the app.

Contact

For questions or deletion requests, use Send feedback in the app or open an issue at github.com/VojnovicSpasoje/gotgotneed/issues.
